## ShotRef 1.7.0

- **See before you click.** Hover a camera move, a motion feel, a lens, a shot size or a pose, and a small moving preview shows what it will do to your shot. Nothing changes until you click. When nothing is in frame, an example scene stands in.
- **A tidier Items list.** Your scene is sorted into groups: People, Vehicles, Buildings, Street lamps, Furniture, Nature, Shapes, Floor marks and Imported. Click a group's name to fold it (ShotRef remembers), or hover it and use its eye to hide the whole group. Selecting something in the view opens its group.
- **Rename fixed.** Double-clicking an item's name in the Items list opens the rename box again.

If you have 1.3.0 or newer installed, ShotRef offers this update by itself (or use **Help, Check for updates…**). On 1.2.0 or older, install this version by hand once.
