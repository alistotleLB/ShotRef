Render style

Video renders frame by frame, so it is always smooth at the chosen FPS, whatever your PC's speed. Helpers like the camera icon and paths are never included.

Quick start

  1. Start from a set (the New button), or click items on the left and then click on the floor to place them.
  2. Block it. Drag people and props across the floor; drag the orange ring to turn them. Top view (T) works like a floor plan.
  3. Move people. Select a person, press Draw walk path and click points on the floor, or send them to a floor mark with an arrival time.
  4. Frame the shot. In Camera view (C) drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to dolly, W A S D Q E to fly. Or use a shot size: Wide, Medium, Close-up…
  5. Make the move. Press Set start, reframe, press Set end. Or pick a one-click move, or ● Record and operate the camera live.
  6. Export an MP4, the current frame, or start + end frames for image-to-video models.
