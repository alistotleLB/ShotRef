Quick start

  1. Start from a set (File then New), or click items on the Add tab on the left and then click on the floor to place them.
  2. Block it. Drag people and props across the floor; drag the orange ring to turn them (let go over a person or the camera and they face it). Shift-click picks several to move together; Ctrl G groups them. Top view (T) works like a floor plan.
  3. Move people. Select a person, press Draw walk path and click points on the floor, or send them to a floor mark with an arrival time. Drag them onto a chair, sofa or bed to sit them down (drag away to stand). Pick a pose later in the shot to change it mid-shot: it shows as a blue tag on the timeline. The eye button over a person makes them look at the camera.
  4. Right-click anything for what you can do with it: add a person here, walk them to a mark, frame a medium shot, face the camera, duplicate, hide. Right-click the floor to add things or aim the camera there. Right-drag still pans.
  5. Frame the shot. In Camera view (C) drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to dolly, W A S D Q E to fly. Or use a shot size: Wide, Medium, Close-up… The split view button (next to View, under the picture) puts the editor on the left and the camera on the right: whichever half the pointer is over is the one you steer.
  6. Make the move. Press Set start, reframe, press Set end. Or pick a one-click move, or ● Record and operate the camera live.
  7. Export (step 3, or press 3): an MP4, the current frame, or a frame pack (start, middle and end frames plus the prompt) for image-to-video models. Several shots? Add them with + New shot.

Step one frame (Shift: 10 frames)
Home End
Go to start / end
F
Zoom the view to the selection
Right-click
Menu for what is under the pointer (the floor, a person, a prop, a mark, the camera)
Double-click
Focus the lens on what you clicked (turns depth of field on). Double-click something else later to pull focus.
Yellow path
Drag a camera point to reshape the move (Alt: up and down). Right-click the path to add a point, a point to delete it. Shows in the editor and top view.
Yellow sun
Drag it to turn the light and the shadows. Right-click it for Front, Side or Backlight, or use the Sun row in the Items list.
Shift-click · Shift-drag
Add or remove one thing in the selection · draw a box on empty space to select what is inside. Ctrl A selects everything.
Ctrl G · Ctrl Shift G
Group the selection (click one to select all, Alt-click for just one) · Ungroup
Ctrl L
Lock or unlock (a locked thing can't be dragged or deleted by accident)
Ctrl D · Del
Duplicate · Delete
Ctrl Z · Ctrl Y

Ctrl S · Ctrl O

P · Ctrl C
