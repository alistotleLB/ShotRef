Quick start

  1. Start from a set (File then New), or click items on the Add tab on the left and then click on the floor to place them.
  2. Block it. Drag people and props across the floor; drag the orange ring to turn them. Top view (T) works like a floor plan.
  3. Move people. Select a person, press Draw walk path and click points on the floor, or send them to a floor mark with an arrival time.
  4. Right-click anything for what you can do with it: add a person here, walk them to a mark, frame a medium shot, face the camera, duplicate, hide. Right-click the floor to add things or aim the camera there. Right-drag still pans.
  5. Frame the shot. In Camera view (C) drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to dolly, W A S D Q E to fly. Or use a shot size: Wide, Medium, Close-up…
  6. Make the move. Press Set start, reframe, press Set end. Or pick a one-click move, or ● Record and operate the camera live.
  7. Export (step 3, or press 3): an MP4, the current frame, or start + end frames for image-to-video models.
